**Nathanaël Machicoane** is a CNRS researcher at the Laboratory of Geophysical and Industrial Flows ([LEGI](www.legi.grenoble-inp.fr/web/)) at the University of Grenoble Alpes. His research focuses mainly on fluid mechanics, in particular multiphase turbulent flows, sprays, atomisation and fragmentation. He studies the transport and heat transfer of particles in anisotropic and inhomogeneous turbulent flows, combining experimental approaches and modelling.
