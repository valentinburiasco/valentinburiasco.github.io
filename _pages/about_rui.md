
**Dr. Ni** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, where he leads the [Fluid Transport Lab](https://me.jhu.edu/fluidtransportlab/). His research focuses on the fundamental science of turbulence and multiphase flows—systems involving more than one phase (liquid, solid, or gas). His work has applications in next-generation energy systems, environmental engineering, and physiological flows in the human body.
