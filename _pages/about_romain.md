[Romain Volk](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/romain.volk/) is a senior lecturer in physics at the École Normale Supérieure de Lyon (**ENS Lyon**), where he works in the Physics Laboratory, in the ‘Waves, Flows and Fluctuations’ team. His research focuses mainly on particle transport in turbulent flows, Lagrangian aspects, turbulent diffusion and modelling. He uses techniques such as particle tracking velocimetry and high-speed visualisation. His work also includes the study of phoretic effects and active systems, with applications in fluid physics and colloid dynamics.
